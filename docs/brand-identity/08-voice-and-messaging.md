## Your Brand Voice

**How does Onéra sound?** This affects every word you write—from the website to a thank you note to a customer.

Think about three to five adjectives that describe your voice. Some examples:

- Poetic vs. direct
- Formal vs. conversational
- Precious vs. understated
- Confident vs. modest
- Lyrical vs. practical

**Questions for you:**

1. **If Onéra were a person, how would she speak?** Fast or slow? With energy or calmness? Simple words or rich vocabulary?
2. **What words or phrases feel like Onéra, and what feels wrong?** (This helps you know what to avoid.)
3. **Do you use the Oxford comma? Exclamation points? Long sentences or short?** These small choices build voice.
4. **How much about the garment's construction do you explain, and how much is implied?** (Detailed & educational vs. poetic & mysterious)

## Core Messages: What You Say

These are the key things people should hear about Onéra:

### One-line description
*To write*

A single sentence that captures what Onéra is. Example: "Timeless garments that move with you." Or: "Locally made, globally inspired eveningwear."

**Your turn:** Write this in your voice. Make it true.

### Short introduction (40–60 words)
*To write*

Tell someone who has never heard of Onéra what you make and why. This goes on your website or in a press email.

### The story (150–250 words)
*To write*

Where did Onéra come from? What drives the work? What do you want someone to feel when they learn about you? This is the medium-length version for a lookbook, interview, or brand page.

### Your tagline or motto
*To decide*

Is it something you've already said? Something new? Does it change by season, or is it permanent? Examples: your existing "Dreams given form" or something entirely new.

### How you describe a specific collection
*To develop each season*

Every season or collection has its own name and story. What's the narrative? What inspired it? What's new or different this time?

### How you describe a single garment
*To develop as you design*

When someone buys a piece, they want to understand it. How do you describe:
- **What it is** (a shirt, a coat, a dress)
- **What it's made of** (the fabric, lining, notions)
- **How it's made** (any special techniques or details)
- **How it feels** (weight, drape, movement)
- **How to wear it** (alone, layered, with what else)
- **Care** (washing, storage, longevity)

Example:
> "The Margot Shirt is cut from Belgian linen in our signature Olive. A relaxed fit with subtle seaming at the shoulder creates gentle movement. The collar softens against the body; the sleeves roll or can be cuffed. Hand wash cool, dry flat."

## Tone by Situation

Your voice might adjust slightly depending on the context:

| Context | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Website & introduction** | *To define* | Inspiring, informative |
| **Garment descriptions** | *To define* | Clear, sensory, honest |
| **Customer care (emails, DMs)** | *To define* | Warm, helpful, human |
| **Press & interviews** | *To define* | Thoughtful, articulate |
| **Social posts** | *To define* | More or less casual? Personal? |

**Questions for you:**

1. **Should your voice be consistent everywhere, or does it shift?** (Many brands are formal on the website but more casual on Instagram.)
2. **What should never happen in your copy?** (Hype-y language? False intimacy? Complicated descriptions?)
3. **When a customer reaches out, how should they feel in your response?**

## Writing Guidelines

Keep these consistent to build brand voice:

- **How you write the brand name:** Onéra (with accent) always? Never "ONÉRA"? Sometimes "onéra" in lower case?
- **Capitalization:** Do you capitalize seasons? Collection names? Garment names?
- **Vocabulary:** Are there words you use repeatedly? Words you avoid?
- **Punctuation:** Em dashes, semicolons, ellipses? What feels right?
- **Sentence structure:** Short and punchy, or longer and flowing?
- **Metaphors & imagery:** Do you use nature imagery? Movement? Light? Craft?

**Questions for you:**

1. **Read your favorite fashion brands' websites.** What words or phrases do they use that feel distinct?
2. **Write a 2–3 sentence product description for something you've already designed.** Does it sound like you?
3. **What's a word or phrase you never want to see in copy about Onéra?** ("Luxury," "flattering," "timeless"—whatever feels off)
