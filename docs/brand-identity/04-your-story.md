## What This Part Is For

These four questions articulate the *why* of Onéra. They're deeper than aesthetics or customer needs—they're about what you believe, where the name came from, and what unique thing only you can contribute to fashion.

This is your origin story. It becomes the thread that runs through everything: your design choices, your voice, your brand decisions, how you talk about your work.

When you can answer these clearly, you have a foundation that nothing can shake.
