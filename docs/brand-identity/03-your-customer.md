## What This Part Is For

These four questions clarify who you're designing for. The more specific you can be about her life, her values, and her needs, the clearer your design decisions become.

Your customer isn't a demographic category. She's a person with a Tuesday. She has preferences, struggles, and desires. The more vivid she is in your mind, the better you'll design for her.
