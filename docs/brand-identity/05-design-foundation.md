## Questions to get started

**Choose one to answer first:**

1. **If you had to describe how an Onéra piece feels in three words, what would they be?**
   - Think about both the physical feeling (weight, drape, texture) and the emotional feeling it creates.

2. **What's the first detail or technique that comes to mind when you think "that's mine, that's Onéra"?**
   - A seam? A proportion? A hem finish? A fabric weight?

3. **Close your eyes and picture your ideal customer wearing something you made. What's she doing? What time of day? What season?**
   - The more specific, the better your design decisions will be.

4. **What would your grandmother or a mentor say is your distinctive gift as a designer?**
   - Sometimes our signature is clearer to others than to ourselves.
