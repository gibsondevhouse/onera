## How to Use These Documents

**Browse first.** Read the intro section of each document to see what's inside. You don't need to answer everything on your first pass.

**Answer what jumps out.** If a question resonates, answer it. If it doesn't, skip it for now.

**Come back when you're stuck.** If you're unsure about a design decision, jump back to the relevant document and see what you said about it.

**Update as you go.** As you answer questions and make decisions, jot notes in the documents. They're working documents—they grow with your thinking.

**Use this as your record.** Six months from now, when you're launching a new collection, these documents remind you why you chose what you chose.

## The Big Picture

Think of this as a conversation between you and your brand:

1. **Steps 2–4** ask you questions about what's already inside you (your aesthetic, customer, story)
2. **Step 5** helps you synthesize those answers into a clear, written brand
3. **Step 6** gives you the launch checklist and tracker
4. **Steps 7–9** are reference docs for building visual identity, voice, and assets
5. **Step 10** is for your development team

You're not trying to fill in everything perfectly. You're building a living document of your thinking.
